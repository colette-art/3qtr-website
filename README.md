# 3QTR Performance Elevate

Create a premium, fancy, modern, and fully responsive website for a sports performance consulting brand called 3QTR.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- The website must have ONLY 5 pages:
  1. Home
  2. About
  3. Services
  4. Who We Serve
  5. Contact
- Do NOT use “LLC” anywhere in the branding.
- The brand name should appear only as: 3QTR
- The website must feel elegant, high-end, strategic, athletic, and professional.
- It must look custom-made, visually impressive, and premium on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Avoid generic sports website design.

BRAND POSITIONING:
3QTR helps competitive sports teams unlock the missing quarter of human performance through assessment-based development focused on communication, motivation, leadership, emotional intelligence, and team cohesion.

MAIN BRAND MESSAGE:
“Unlocking the 4th Quarter of Human Performance”

DESIGN DIRECTION:
- Premium and bold
- Executive consulting meets elite sports performance
- Fancy but clean
- Dark luxury-inspired design
- Smooth animations and polished section transitions
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Beautiful responsive layouts for all screen sizes

COLOR STYLE:
- Deep navy background
- Rich gold accents
- White / off-white text
- Light gray support backgrounds for contrast sections

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Strong, authoritative headline font
- Clean sans-serif body font
- Elegant and modern spacing
- Large, impactful hero headline

SITE STRUCTURE:

PAGE 1 — HOME
This page should contain the most important content from the business and act as the main selling page.

Include these sections on the Home page:
1. Hero Section
   - Brand name: 3QTR
   - Headline: Unlocking the 4th Quarter of Human Performance
   - Subheadline: Assessment-Based Performance Solutions for Competitive Sports Teams
   - CTA buttons: Book a Discovery Call / Learn More

2. Problem / Opportunity Section
   Explain that most teams invest in:
   - physical training
   - tactical preparation
   but often miss the human side of performance:
   - communication
   - motivation
   - emotional intelligence
   - leadership
   - team cohesion

3. The 3QTR Solution
   Present 3QTR as the missing quarter that helps athletes and coaches perform better together.

4. Founder Highlights Preview
   Introduce Colette M. Hosie with a premium summary:
   - Athlete & Engineer
   - NCAA-certified AAU coach
   - 29-year Fortune 500 executive
   - Hall of Fame team member
   - Triple-certified in DISC, Indigo, and EQ

5. Services Overview
   Add premium cards previewing:
   - Student-Athlete Profile
   - Coaching Staff Development
   - The 3QTR Edge
   - Season Retainer

6. Who We Serve Preview
   - Varsity High School Programs
   - College Programs
   - AAU & Club Sports
   - Parents of student-athletes

7. Why 3QTR
   Create strong credibility/value blocks:
   - assessment-based development
   - customized solutions
   - leadership and team communication focus
   - athlete + executive + coaching experience

8. Final CTA Section
   Headline example:
   “Let’s talk about what your team is missing.”
   Include strong contact button and consultation CTA.

PAGE 2 — ABOUT
Focus fully on Colette M. Hosie and the story behind 3QTR.

Include:
- founder story
- experience in sports, coaching, leadership, and corporate performance
- certifications

- quote block

Make this page feel authoritative, inspiring, and premium.

Make “The 3QTR Edge” the featured service card.
